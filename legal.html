Terms of use

By creating an account or using Daily Brick, you agree to these terms. If you don't agree, please don't use the app.

1. What Daily Brick is

Daily Brick is an online game where people place one virtual brick a day on a shared wall, with the goal of building a wall longer than the Great Wall of China. The bricks are virtual items inside the app. They have no cash value, can't be exchanged for money, and are not an investment.

2. Your account

  • You need an account to place bricks. Give a real email address and keep your password private.
  • Daily Brick is for people aged 18 and over. Younger users may only use it with permission from a parent or guardian, who accepts these terms for them.
  • You're responsible for what happens on your account. Tell us straight away if you think someone else is using it.
  • You can delete your account at any time from the Me screen in the app.

3. What you put on the wall

Your name, the writing on your bricks, photos and business logos are seen by everyone who uses the app. Only add things you have the right to share. Don't add anything that is:

  • rude, hateful, threatening, sexual or violent;
  • someone else's photo, logo or trademark without their permission;
  • personal information about another person, such as their phone number or address;
  • spam, scams, or links to harmful content.

Photos and logos are checked before others can see them. We may remove any writing, photo, logo or brick, or close an account, that breaks these rules, without a refund for that item. You keep ownership of what you upload, and you give Daily Brick permission to show it in the app and in promotional images of the wall.

4. Paid extras and the business plan

  • The daily brick, its colour, writing on it and streaks are free.
  • Some extras cost money, such as special finishes, photos on bricks and streak freezes. The business plan gives up to 10 bricks a day and a logo banner for 30 days per payment.
  • Prices are shown in South African rand (ZAR) before you pay. If your card is in another currency, your bank converts it and may charge a fee.
  • Payments are handled by Paystack. Daily Brick never sees or stores your card number.
  • Nothing renews automatically. The business plan ends after 30 days unless you pay again.
  • Free rewards for streaks are a thank-you, not something you are owed, and we may change them in future.

5. Fair play

Don't try to place more bricks than allowed, use several accounts to get around limits, copy the app, attack it, or use bots or scripts with it. We may suspend accounts that do.

6. The app is offered as it is

We work hard to keep Daily Brick running, but we can't promise it will always be available or free of errors. Streaks and bricks may occasionally be affected by technical problems; if that happens, contact us and we'll try to put it right. As far as the law allows, Daily Brick is not liable for indirect losses, and our total liability to you is limited to the amount you paid us in the 3 months before the problem. Nothing in these terms takes away rights you have under the Consumer Protection Act or other South African law.

Privacy policy

This policy explains what personal information Daily Brick collects, why, and what you can do about it, in line with South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA). Carel Combrink is the responsible party and the Information Officer.

What we collect

  • Account details: your email address, the name you choose to show, and your password (stored only in scrambled, encrypted form).
  • Google or Facebook sign-in: if you sign in with Google or Facebook, we receive your name, email address and an account ID from them. We use your first name on your bricks unless you change it. We don't get your password or post anything for you.
  • Your socials: if you add your Instagram, TikTok or Facebook name, it is shown to people who tap your bricks, with a link to your profile. You can remove it at any time on the Me screen.
  • What you add: your bricks, their colours, writing, photos, and for businesses the business name, logo and website.
  • Game activity: when you place bricks, your streaks, rewards and streak freezes.
  • Payments: what you bought, the amount and the date. Card details go straight to Paystack and are never seen or stored by Daily Brick.
  • Reminders: if you turn on daily reminders, a technical address for your phone or browser and the time you chose.
  • Technical information: basic logs kept by our hosting providers, such as IP address and browser type, used to keep the service secure.

Why we use it

  • to run your account and the wall, and keep your streak and rewards;
  • to process payments and keep a record of them;
  • to send you the daily reminder, only if you switched it on;
  • to check photos and logos, prevent abuse and keep the app secure;
  • to understand how the app is used (as totals, not to track you) and improve it.

We don't sell your personal information, and we don't show third-party ads.

Who else handles it

Daily Brick uses trusted service providers to run the app. They process information only to provide their service:

  • Supabase: database, accounts and photo storage;
  • Vercel: hosting of the website;
  • Paystack: card payments;
  • Google and Facebook (Meta): only if you choose to sign in with them;
  • Google, Apple or Mozilla: delivering reminder notifications to your phone or browser;
  • Google Fonts: loading the app's lettering.

Some of these providers store information on servers outside South Africa. They are bound by data protection laws and agreements that give a level of protection similar to POPIA. We may also share information if the law requires it.

What everyone can see

Your display name, the writing on your bricks, approved photos, business names, approved logos and streak badges are visible to everyone using the app. Your email address and payment details are never shown to other users.

How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account is open. When you delete your account, your profile, bricks, photos, logos and reminders are deleted straight away. Payment records held by Paystack are kept by them as the law requires.

Information is sent over encrypted connections, access is protected by database security rules, and passwords are never stored in readable form. No system is perfect; if a breach affecting your information ever happens, we'll tell you and the Information Regulator as POPIA requires.
